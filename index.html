About the College

Rongjeng Model Degree College is located at the foothills of Darugre, Rongjeng. The College started in the year 2024 and the commencement of academic session was inaugurated on August 19th 2024 by Hon’ble Chief Minister Shri Conrad K. Sangma, Hon’ble Education Minister Shri Rakkam A. Sangma along with Hon’ble MLA, Jim M. Sangma.

Many dignitaries were involved during the inauguration of the academic session. The College has been a milestone achievement for the Garo Hills to access quality education and pursue their ambitions.

The College comprises of administrative unit and many classrooms, library room, student common room for both boys and girls, teacher common room and other auxiliary units to support the academic block. The College also provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls with a reliable supply of water facilities. Additionally, the college is surrounded by natural trees and forest that offers a perfect bliss for learning.

At present, the college is led by a principal and is supported by faculty members along with other non-teaching staff, all of whom are dedicated to providing quality education for the betterment of society.

In January 2025, the college received affiliation from Captain Williamson Sangma State University, aiming to address students’ concerns regarding admissions and to facilitate their pursuit of higher education.
