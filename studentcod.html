Student Code of Conduct

Discipline

  • Every student must reach the college campus on time for assembly.
  • Students are expected to follow a proper dress code within the campus and the classrooms.
  • Use of mobile phones during class hours is prohibited and liable for punishment.
  • Intoxication of betel nuts, alcohol, smoking and any substance abuse within the campus or classrooms are strictly prohibited and liable for punishment.
  • Students must wear their I.D cards regularly in the college campus.
  • Any sort of misconduct or misbehaviour towards lecturers or non-teaching staffs is strictly prohibited and repercussions shall be initiated.
  • Students must refrain from mishandling and tampering with library books.
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited in the college. Disciplinary action shall be imposed on those who violate the rule.

Academic Conduct

  • Regular attendance and active participation in classes are mandatory. Students must accomplish the required attendance percentage of 90% to sit for examinations.
  • Students must inform their respective Academic Councillors in case they are absent and bring the Leave Application the next day.
  • Students must submit assignments and presentations on time as required by the courses.
  • Students must refrain from cheating and unfair means such as copying during examinations and replicating homework, assignments, and projects.
  • The Class Representatives are responsible for maintaining class decorum during the absence of lecturers.
  • Students are expected to participate in extracurricular activities to enhance personality, communication and soft skills.

Campus Conduct

  • Students shall not spit or throw garbage, plastics or paper within the college premises.
  • Students are expected to maintain cleanliness of the college campus.
  • Any sort of insolent behaviour or attitude on the campus is strictly prohibited and liable for necessary action.
  • Plucking and tweaking of plants and trees are strictly prohibited on the campus.
  • Loitering around the campus during class hours is strictly prohibited.
